# 📚 **Therapy Catalog for Self-Assessment Journey Website**

---

## 🧠 1. **Cognitive & Behavioral Therapies (CBT Family)**

* **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)**

  * Restructure negative thoughts → balanced alternatives.
  * **Digital exercises**: thought records, cognitive reframing cards.

* **Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP)** *(gold standard for OCD)*

  * Gradual exposure to feared thoughts/situations → resist rituals.
  * **Digital exercises**: exposure hierarchy builder, trigger diary.

* **Behavioral Activation (BA)**

  * Replace avoidance with meaningful activities.
  * **Digital exercises**: activity scheduling planner, mood tracking.

* **Habit Reversal Training (HRT)**

  * Replace compulsions/tics with alternative actions.
  * **Digital exercises**: habit loop tracker, competing response prompts.

* **Problem-Solving Therapy (PST)**

  * Structured steps to break down problems and generate solutions.
  * **Digital exercises**: 5-step problem solver tool.

---

## 🌱 2. **Mindfulness & Acceptance Therapies**

* **Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)**

  * Meditation, body scans, mindful awareness.
  * **Digital exercises**: 3-min breathing space, grounding animations.

* **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)**

  * Accept intrusive thoughts, commit to values-driven action.
  * **Digital exercises**: “Leaves on a Stream” visualization, values card sorting.

* **Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT)**

  * Blend of mindfulness + CBT (esp. relapse prevention in depression).
  * **Digital exercises**: mindful journaling, thought observation tasks.

---

## 🔥 3. **Emotion Regulation & Interpersonal Therapies**

* **Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)** *(great for anger, mood swings)*

  * Emotional regulation, distress tolerance, interpersonal effectiveness.
  * **Digital exercises**: STOP skill simulator, opposite action prompts.

* **Anger Management Therapy**

  * Identify triggers, practice relaxation, cognitive reframing.
  * **Digital exercises**: anger diary, trigger-response chain builder.

* **Interpersonal Therapy (IPT)**

  * Focus on relationships and communication.
  * **Digital exercises**: role-play simulations, communication reflection logs.

* **Emotion-Focused Therapy (EFT)**

  * Identify, process, and transform emotional experiences.
  * **Digital exercises**: emotion labeling wheel, self-expression prompts.

---

## 💙 4. **Compassion & Self-Kindness Therapies**

* **Compassion-Focused Therapy (CFT)**

  * Reduce shame, increase self-kindness.
  * **Digital exercises**: self-compassion letter writing, guided compassion meditations.

* **Positive Psychology Interventions (PPI)**

  * Focus on strengths, gratitude, optimism.
  * **Digital exercises**: gratitude journaling, savoring diary, “three good things”.

* **Strength-Based Therapy**

  * Highlight personal strengths for resilience.
  * **Digital exercises**: VIA strengths quiz integration, personal affirmations builder.

---

## 🌙 5. **Lifestyle & Holistic Approaches**

* **Sleep Hygiene Therapy**

  * Structured routines for quality sleep.
  * **Digital exercises**: sleep log, bedtime routine checklist.

* **Relaxation Training**

  * Progressive muscle relaxation, guided imagery.
  * **Digital exercises**: audio scripts, body scan animations.

* **Breathing Techniques**

  * Box breathing, 4-7-8 technique.
  * **Digital exercises**: breathing pacer animation.

* **Exercise & Movement Therapy**

  * Incorporating physical activity to improve mental health.
  * **Digital exercises**: daily movement challenge reminders.

* **Nutrition & Mind-Body Wellness** *(supportive, not prescriptive)*

  * Balanced diet + mood correlation.
  * **Digital exercises**: food-mood journaling.

---

## 🌀 6. **Specialized Therapies (Advanced Modules)**

* **Eye Movement Desensitization & Reprocessing (EMDR)** *(for trauma, PTSD)*

  * Bilateral stimulation while processing distressing memories.
  * **Digital adaptation**: guided visualization + bilateral sound taps.

* **Schema Therapy**

  * Identify deep-rooted patterns (“schemas”) and heal them.
  * **Digital exercises**: schema identification quiz, reparenting exercises.

* **Narrative Therapy**

  * Reframe life story to empower user.
  * **Digital exercises**: “rewrite your story” guided journaling.

* **Motivational Interviewing (MI)**

  * Resolve ambivalence, enhance motivation.
  * **Digital exercises**: decisional balance worksheet (pros/cons).

* **Solution-Focused Therapy (SFT)**

  * Build on what’s working, small wins.
  * **Digital exercises**: miracle question prompts, goal scaling.

---

## 🌍 7. **Community & Long-Term Engagement**

* **Peer Support Elements** (optional future expansion)

  * Anonymous sharing boards (with moderation).

* **Daily Micro-Interventions**

  * 1–2 min tasks: grounding, gratitude, thought reframing.

* **Gamification**

  * Progress badges, streak tracking, therapy level unlocking.
